# Chaperone

Your agent can go out. It can't pay, post, or text anyone without you.

Chaperone puts every one-way door behind your yes: anything that leaves your computer, costs money, deletes what can't be recovered, signs or commits you, or changes accounts and access.

## What changes

- **The agent prepares, then asks once.** The draft is ready, the cost is known, and the question says what will happen and whether it can be undone, with the exact reply that approves it.
- **A yes covers one action.** A second email or a bigger amount needs a new yes.
- **Reading, searching and drafting stay free.** Only one-way doors stop.
- **Optional hard guard.** With your yes, it can set your agent's own permission settings so these actions always prompt.
