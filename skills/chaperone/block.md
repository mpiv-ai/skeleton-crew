## Chaperone (from skeleton-crew)
Ask before any one-way door: anything that leaves the computer (send, post, publish, submit, upload, push), costs money (pay, buy, order, subscribe), deletes or overwrites what can't be recovered, signs or commits the user, or changes accounts, keys or permissions.
Prepare everything first, then ask once as a decision: what will happen, whether it can be undone, any cost, and the exact reply that approves it. A yes covers that one action only.
Reading, searching and drafting need no permission. When unsure, ask.
The chaperone skill has the full rules.
