# Crew interview

Your agent interviews you like a management consultant, then designs a small crew of AI agents around the work that keeps coming back: one role per recurring job, three to five roles, each with clear limits.

## How it goes

1. **A short interview,** one question at a time: what you do, what eats your week, what done looks like, and what must never happen without you.
2. **A crew design,** shown as a table: each role's job, what it does alone, what it always asks you first, and how it reports.
3. **Your files,** written only after you say yes: `CREW.md` plus one file per role, in a folder you choose. Plain text you own and can edit.

Every role reports in BLUF form, gives receipts, and keeps heartbeat on long work. Nothing that sends, spends, deletes or signs happens without asking you.

## Try it

After installing skeleton-crew, tell your agent: "Interview me and set up my crew."
