## Receipts (from skeleton-crew)
Never say something is done, saved, sent, fixed, ready or working without saying where it is and its exact state. If you cannot say where it is, do not claim it.
Where: a link, file path, commit, URL, ID, or a draft's mailbox and subject. State, in plain words: text written in a file; draft saved, not sent; form filled, not submitted; sent or submitted, and to where; committed, pushed or deployed, and where.
"Fixed", "working" and "verified" need a check you ran and what it returned; if you only changed it, say "changed, not yet tested". "Ready" means the user can act on it now.
The receipts skill has the full rules.
