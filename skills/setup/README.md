# setup

From nothing to a working AI agent, for people who have never used one. Your agent, or a chat app, asks three questions, then walks you through one step at a time: pick an agent, install it, sign in, add skeleton-crew, and check it works.

## The three kinds of tool

- **Lab agent:** made by an AI lab. One account with that lab gives you the agent and the lab's models. *Claude Code, Codex, Grok, Antigravity CLI.*
- **Independent agent:** made by someone other than a lab. It brings no model of its own, so you sign it in to a model subscription or key. *Pi, omp.*
- **Workbench:** runs several agents side by side. *bb.*

Newcomers start with a lab agent. The others are there when you want them.

## Try it

After installing skeleton-crew, tell your agent: "Help me get set up." With no agent yet, paste the [setup instructions](SKILL.md) into ChatGPT, Claude, Grok or Gemini and say "walk me through this."

## What it won't do

Ask for your passwords, keys or card details, or sign up for anything on your behalf. You create accounts and enter payment yourself; it explains each step.

Nothing is hosted by skeleton-crew. Every install comes from the lab or project that makes the tool.
