# skeleton-crew

skeleton-crew is a free, open-source set of skills that make AI agents brief you like a professional staff: the answer first, decisions you can approve in a word or two, and claims you can check. It comes from MPIV Labs, the open-source side of MPIsaac Ventures (MPIV).

## Why it exists

Agents are powerful and the landscape is confusing: labs, models, apps, independent agents, workbenches, subscriptions and keys. skeleton-crew packages the working habits of a real crew of agents that runs a one-person company every day, so anyone can get the same results without learning the field first.

- **Plain language.** It explains each choice when the choice comes up, not before.
- **Built in public.** Every change is a public commit at github.com/mpiv-ai/skeleton-crew.
- **Nothing hosted.** skeleton-crew runs no servers and never sees your data, accounts or keys. Every install comes from the maker of each tool.
- **MIT licence.** Free to use, copy and change.

## The skills

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| `setup` | From nothing to a working agent: checks what you have, then one step at a time. |
| `bluf` | Bottom line up front: the answer first, decisions you approve in a word or two. |
| `receipts` | No claim of done, sent or fixed without where it is and its exact state. |
| `heartbeat` | Never wait blind: every wait bounded, checked and reported. |

More skills are added as they prove themselves.

## Look after it

- **Check what's on:** `check.sh` (Mac, Linux) or `check.ps1` (Windows) from the repository.
- **Update:** run the installer again; it upgrades in place.
- **Turn off:** `uninstall.sh` or `uninstall.ps1`; it removes only what it added.
- **Report a problem or suggest a skill:** open an issue at github.com/mpiv-ai/skeleton-crew/issues.
